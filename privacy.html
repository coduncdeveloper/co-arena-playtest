Iron Oath

Privacy Notice

Last updated: June 11, 2026

Iron Oath collects the minimum account data needed for optional accounts, profiles, run telemetry, and leaderboards. Anonymous guest play stays local in your browser/device and does not post run telemetry.

What is collected

  • Email address through Supabase Auth (email sign-in), or your SteamID and public Steam persona name when signing in through Steam.
  • Username.
  • Optional bio.
  • Avatar choice.
  • Signed-in run outcomes and run-summary data: oath, outcome, map reached, duration, scars, mastery threshold, daily-challenge date, and completion time.

What is public

Leaderboard and profile surfaces may show your username, bio, avatar, run stats, and recent run summaries. Email addresses are never shown publicly.

Retention

Account data is retained until the account is deleted. Local guest saves, local run history, settings, and unlock progress remain on your device unless you clear local data during the deletion flow or clear browser storage yourself.

Processor

Supabase processes authentication, profile rows, run records, daily seeds, and leaderboard views. Steam sign-in is handled through Valve's Steam OpenID service; the game receives only your public SteamID and persona name from Valve — never your Steam password or payment details.

Export and delete

Signed-in users can open Account Settings in the game to export their profile and run data as JSON, and to delete their account. Deletion removes the public profile row and signed-in run records, and attempts to delete the Supabase Auth user via the configured database function; if the project does not permit that, contact the owner for final Auth removal. Full steps: Delete Your Account.
