Iron Oath

Delete Your Account

Last updated: June 23, 2026

This page explains how to delete your Iron Oath account and exactly what data is removed. Account deletion is permanent. (Playing as a guest does not create an account — guest progress lives only in your browser/device and is never uploaded.)

Delete from within the game (fastest)

  1. Open Iron Oath and make sure you're signed in.
  2. Go to Account (the profile button), then the Account tab.
  3. Under Danger Zone, optionally tick "Clear local saves and settings after deletion," then press Delete Account.
  4. Press Delete Account again to confirm. Your account and data are removed immediately and you are signed out.

You can also export a copy of your data first via Export Data on the same screen.

No longer have the game installed?

Email coduncdeveloper@gmail.com from (or naming) the email address on your account, with the subject "Iron Oath account deletion request." We will delete the account and confirm by reply.

What gets deleted

  • Your public profile row — username, bio, avatar, and equipped cosmetics.
  • All of your signed-in run records and run-summary data (oath, outcome, map reached, duration, scars, mastery, daily-challenge dates, completion times).
  • Your gameplay telemetry events tied to the account.
  • Your authentication record (the Supabase Auth user / email sign-in), where the database permits it. If automatic auth-record removal is blocked, we complete it manually on request.
  • Optionally, your on-device saves, settings, and unlock progress — only if you tick "Clear local saves" during deletion (or clear your browser storage yourself).

What we keep

Nothing that identifies you. After deletion there is no profile, no run history, and no telemetry tied to your account. We do not sell data or retain personal data for marketing.

How long it takes

In-app deletion is immediate. Email requests are completed within 30 days (typically much sooner).
